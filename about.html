About Trackora

A frontend concept for a premium GPS tracking brand.

Our concept

Tracking that feels premium

Trackora is a design and frontend demonstration of how a modern GPS tracking brand could look: clear product pages, a smooth shop experience and a tidy tracking portal.

This phase includes no backend, no real GPS devices, no real maps, no payments and no authentication. Those will be added later.
