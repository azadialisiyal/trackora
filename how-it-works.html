How It Works

A walkthrough of the intended Trackora experience — frontend preview only.

1

Choose your tracker

Browse the catalogue and compare sample vehicle, mini, personal, magnetic and asset trackers.

2

Add to cart (demo)

Use the cart to see how quantities and subtotals would work. Checkout is a demo only.

3

Pair in the portal (concept)

In the portal preview you can add sample devices to see how pairing could feel.

4

Monitor and manage

View sample positions on an illustrative map, review alerts and browse history.

5

Adjust settings

Switch alert preferences and units in the settings preview.
